CASE STUDY 01

Caremeez — AI Content Generation & Multi-Platform Publishing Engine

An end-to-end AI content operations workflow designed to generate healthcare content, prevent topic duplication, publish across LinkedIn and Facebook, and track publishing history automatically.

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

From manual publishing to a structured content pipeline

During my AI Automation Internship at Caremeez, I designed and built an n8n-based automation for the company's healthcare content publishing process.

The system connected content ideation, duplicate detection, AI content generation, multi-platform publishing, and publishing-status tracking into one repeatable workflow.

THE PROBLEM

The publishing process was largely manual

Caremeez was manually preparing and publishing healthcare-related social media content. Maintaining consistency required recurring manual effort, while there was no structured pipeline for managing topics, generated content, publishing, and content history.

01

Recurring manual effort

Content preparation and publishing had to be repeatedly handled manually.

02

No structured content pipeline

Topic ideation, generation, publishing, and content history were not connected through one automated system.

03

Repetition risk

Previously used topics needed to be tracked to avoid repeatedly generating similar content.

THE SOLUTION

An end-to-end AI content operations engine

Instead of automating only the final publishing step, I designed the workflow as a complete content operations pipeline.

WORKFLOW

How the automation works

01

Scheduled execution

The workflow starts through a configurable schedule based on the required publishing frequency.

02

AI topic generation

OpenAI generates a fresh healthcare topic designed around relevant and relatable healthcare discussions for an Indian audience.

03

Duplicate prevention

Previously generated topics are retrieved from Google Sheets and compared with the new topic. Duplicate topics are rejected.

04

AI content generation

A second AI stage generates a structured healthcare awareness post with a human, relatable tone, relevant insights and hashtags.

05

Content tracking

Google Sheets stores the generated topic, content and publishing status, creating a lightweight content history.

06

Multi-platform publishing

The generated content is published to the Caremeez LinkedIn Organization Page and Facebook Page through their respective APIs.

DEPLOYMENT CHALLENGE

The automation was technically complete, but platform access became the bottleneck

The primary deployment challenge was related to Facebook Page authentication and access tokens. The initial credentials did not provide the required Page-level access, and the Facebook access token later expired without a replacement Page token being generated.

As a result, the Facebook publishing component could not continue operating reliably. This highlighted an important production lesson: automation reliability depends not only on workflow logic, but also on external permissions, credentials, and authentication lifecycle management.

ENGINEERING LEARNINGS

What this project taught me

01

Automate the workflow, not just the task

The real value came from connecting ideation, validation, generation, publishing, and tracking.

02

AI needs system-level controls

Structured prompts, topic constraints, duplicate detection, and output requirements make AI workflows more reliable.

03

APIs are part of the engineering problem

Authentication, permissions, tokens, and platform requirements can directly affect production reliability.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

From Generate → Validate → Create → Publish → Track

This project transformed a recurring manual social media activity into a structured, scheduled, and largely autonomous content operations workflow. More importantly, it provided hands-on experience with real platform APIs, authentication, live publishing, workflow testing, and deployment constraints.
