CASE STUDY 02

The Original Tutors — AI Lead Conversion & Demo Engagement System

A structured lead-conversion system designed to capture coaching enquiries, understand student intent, qualify leads, follow up, and move suitable prospects toward a demo.

n8n AI / LLM WhatsApp Google Sheets Notion
Role AI Automation Engineer / System Designer
Project Type AI Lead Conversion
Primary Module Enquiry → Demo
Project Status Architecture Validated Through Pilot

PROJECT SNAPSHOT

Turning incoming enquiries into structured conversations

The Original Tutors receives student enquiries through channels such as Instagram advertising. The challenge was not simply receiving those enquiries, but responding quickly, understanding student requirements, answering questions, identifying genuine interest, and moving suitable leads toward a demo.

I designed an AI-powered lead-conversion system around the larger coaching-business flow:

Enquiry
→
Demo
→
Enrollment
→
Payment

This project specifically focused on Module 1 — Enquiry → Demo.

THE PROBLEM

Every enquiry required a conversation before it could become a demo

A new student enquiry is not automatically ready for enrollment. The conversation typically needs to move through several stages before a genuine prospect reaches a demo.

New Enquiry
→
Initial Response
→
Understand Requirement
→
Answer Questions
→
Identify Interest
→
Demo

As enquiry volume increases, manually managing every conversation becomes difficult. The system was therefore designed to make lead handling structured, repeatable, and intent-driven.

SYSTEM OBJECTIVE

Define a next action for every enquiry

01

Respond quickly

Reduce the delay between a new enquiry and the initial response.

02

Understand the student

Collect relevant information and understand the student's requirement.

03

Qualify interest

Identify whether the lead is highly interested, needs more information, or shows low interest.

04

Route intelligently

Handle routine conversations while routing teacher-dependent questions for human intervention.

05

Move interested leads toward a demo

Provide relevant demo information or scheduling guidance when genuine interest is identified.

06

Maintain lead continuity

Track lead information and follow up when an engaged enquiry stops responding.

SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE

A modular lead-conversion workflow

Instead of building a simple chatbot, I designed the system as a modular lead-conversion process.

HOW IT WORKS

From enquiry to demo

01

New lead capture

An incoming enquiry is captured and converted into a structured lead record with a unique Lead ID.

02

Personalised initial response

The first response is designed to start a conversation and collect the information required to understand the enquiry.

03

Conversational qualification

AI-assisted logic analyses student responses and helps determine the appropriate next action.

04

Intent-based routing

Interested leads move toward demo information, questions can be routed for human resolution, and low-interest leads can move toward an appropriate close.

05

Follow-up

The designed architecture includes a 1-hour engagement follow-up and a 24-hour no-reply reminder.

06

Demo transition

Suitable interested students are moved toward the demo and handed over to the teacher for the next stage.

WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE

n8n lead-conversion workflow

The workflow architecture combines lead capture, AI-assisted understanding, conversational routing, tracking, follow-up, and human escalation.

WORKFLOW SCREENSHOT

PILOT EXECUTION

The architecture was validated through a supervised real-lead pilot

The complete architecture was designed for automated execution. For the pilot, however, I manually executed the workflow logic under supervision.

This approach allowed the client to experience the intended lead-conversion process without requiring the operating cost of a complete production automation during the pilot.

01

Initial response

A predefined personalised response was sent to each incoming enquiry.

02

AI-assisted response

Student responses were reviewed and AI was used to help generate appropriate conversational responses.

03

Human escalation

Questions requiring teacher involvement were assigned accordingly.

04

Demo transition

Interested students were moved toward the demo and added to the relevant course group as instructed by the teacher.

PILOT RESULTS

Observed results from the real-lead pilot

18 Leads handled
7 Replies received
5 Meaningful / warm conversations
5 Demo-interested students
2 Demo attendances observed

The pilot ran for approximately 3–4 days. These figures represent observed pilot activity and are not presented as a controlled measurement of conversion-rate improvement.

KEY PILOT OBSERVATIONS

What the pilot revealed

01

Low-intent enquiries were common

Not every advertising lead was genuinely interested, making qualification important.

02

Personalisation mattered

Conversational and personalised responses were more useful than simply sending generic promotional information.

03

Response speed mattered

The workflow was designed around quick initial responses to fresh enquiries.

04

Structured tracking improved visibility

Lead status became easier to distinguish across new enquiries, active conversations, interested leads, demo opportunities, and follow-ups.

ENGINEERING LEARNINGS

What this project taught me

01

A lead-conversion system is more than a chatbot

The system needs to understand where a lead is in the conversion journey and determine the next appropriate action.

02

Qualification is essential

Intent-based routing helps focus attention according to lead behaviour.

03

Human intervention needs a defined place

Not every enquiry should be handled entirely by AI. Teacher-dependent questions can be escalated.

04

Pilots can validate architecture

A supervised manual pilot can test workflow logic with real leads before investing in full production infrastructure.

05

Modular systems scale better

Separating Enquiry → Demo → Enrollment → Payment makes the system easier to maintain and extend.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

Every enquiry should have a defined next action

This project transformed lead handling from a sequence of manual messages into a structured, intent-driven conversion process for coaching enquiries.

The supervised pilot handled 18 actual enquiries and provided practical validation of the designed lead-conversion logic, while establishing the foundation for future Demo → Enrollment and Payment Follow-up modules.
