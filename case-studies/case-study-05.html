PROJECT STATUS

Designed and documented as a realistic proposal automation architecture

The workflow was designed as a six-node n8n architecture using OpenAI for proposal generation and Google Sheets, Gmail and Notion for tracking, delivery and storage.

The workflow was not connected to production credentials or tested with real client data. Therefore, no production performance, conversion or revenue claims are made.

THE PROBLEM

Proposal creation becomes repetitive when handling multiple prospects

Creating a professional proposal can involve collecting requirements, reviewing budget and timelines, structuring scope and deliverables, writing the proposal, formatting it, sending it and maintaining an internal record.

Gather the information required to understand the project.

02

03

Create the proposal

Convert structured requirements into a professional proposal.

04

Deliver and record it

Send the proposal and maintain an internal history for tracking.

ENGINEERING LEARNINGS

What this project demonstrates

01

AI generation needs structured inputs

Cleaning and standardizing client information improves the consistency of the generation stage.

02

AI should be surrounded by deterministic workflow logic

OpenAI generates the proposal while n8n controls data flow, logging, delivery and storage.

03

Proposal automation is more than content generation

A practical system also needs tracking, delivery and storage.

04

Record-keeping belongs in the workflow

Automatically storing proposal information creates a useful history for future follow-ups and reporting.

05

Modular workflows are easier to expand

The architecture can later support PDF generation, CRM synchronization, approvals, notifications and follow-up automation.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

Capture → Clean → Generate → Log → Deliver → Store

The AI Proposal Generation System demonstrates how AI can be integrated into a complete business workflow instead of being used only for text generation.

The architecture connects client input, data preparation, AI proposal generation, logging, email delivery and internal storage while remaining modular enough for future CRM, PDF, approval and follow-up integrations.

This project is presented as a designed and documented automation architecture, not as a production deployment or a system with measured business results.
