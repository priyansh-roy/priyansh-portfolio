THE PROBLEM

Outbound sales contains many connected manual processes

Manual outbound sales can involve finding prospects, researching them, writing messages, sending outreach, monitoring replies, understanding intent, following up, qualifying leads and updating CRM records.

Different replies also require different actions: interested prospects, pricing questions, general questions, people requesting more information, uninterested prospects and spam should not all receive the same response.

HIGH-LEVEL ARCHITECTURE

Eight connected layers

01

Outreach Batch Engine

Fetches, validates and organizes prospects into controlled outreach batches.

02

AI Email Personalization

Generates prospect-specific subjects, email bodies, business context and structured HTML output.

03

Reply Listener & Preprocessing

Detects campaign replies, filters unrelated messages and prepares responses for classification.

04

AI Reply Classification

Determines whether a reply represents interest, pricing questions, general questions, rejection, spam or another relevant intent.

05

WhatsApp Handoff

Moves meaningful warm leads from email conversations toward a WhatsApp-based sales interaction.

06

AI WhatsApp SDR

Handles multi-turn conversations, requirements, questions, objections and lead qualification.

07

Follow-up Engine

Re-engages prospects who have not responded while checking lead state before continuing.

08

CRM & Reporting

Synchronizes qualified leads, deal summaries, operational records and reporting information.

ENGINEERING LEARNINGS

What this project taught me

01

Sales automation should be treated as a system

Outreach, replies, qualification and CRM management are connected processes.

02

AI works best with deterministic logic

AI handles personalization, classification and responses while workflow logic controls routing, timing, storage and synchronization.

03

Human escalation matters

Practical AI sales systems need clear boundaries for when a human should take over.

04

Follow-up requires state awareness

A follow-up should check whether the prospect has already responded before continuing.

05

Modular architecture simplifies complexity

Breaking the system into layers makes development, troubleshooting and future deployment more manageable.

FINAL TAKEAWAY

From prospecting to qualification in one connected architecture

This project demonstrates an approach to end-to-end AI sales automation rather than isolated AI workflows.

The architecture connects prospecting, personalized outreach, reply intelligence, lead nurturing, WhatsApp SDR conversations, qualification, follow-up, CRM and reporting into one modular system.

The project is presented as a designed and documented automation architecture. Production validation remains pending because live credentials and real prospect testing were not available.
